# Project Wayfinder — Addendum: Exploration Society Theme

## Exploration Society
The player is the Director of a young, underfunded Victorian Exploration Society competing against prestigious rival organizations. The long-term goal is to build the world's foremost institution of exploration through successful expeditions, discoveries, diplomacy, and scientific achievement.

### Prestige
Prestige is the Society's permanent measure of reputation rather than a spendable currency. It reflects scientific recognition, investor confidence, government trust, and public admiration. Expeditions earn Prestige by making first discoveries, recovering significant artifacts, documenting cultures, and completing prestigious commissions.

### Rival Societies
Competing societies continue exploring the world independently. They may claim first discoveries, establish outposts, or publish scientific breakthroughs, creating organic pressure.

### Headquarters
The headquarters grows into a world-class institution with departments such as:
- Museum Wing
- Geographic Survey Office
- Scientific Academy
- Anthropological Society
- Engineering Corps

Artifacts become museum exhibits that reinforce the Society's reputation.

## Intelligence Network

The belief map is expanded with an Intelligence layer. Rather than revealing terrain directly, intelligence sources provide imperfect clues that increase or decrease confidence in regional hypotheses.

Potential sources include:
- Newspapers
- Tribal knowledge
- Expedition journals
- Rival expedition reports
- Animal migration
- Geological samples
- Weather observations

Each contributes a small weighted modifier to belief confidence.

## Outpost Infrastructure

Outposts are no longer only launch locations. They may construct specialized facilities.

### Weather Balloon Station

Weather balloons gather atmospheric observations over time.

They do **not** reveal terrain directly.

Instead they improve confidence in the belief model by observing:
- prevailing winds
- rainfall
- rain shadows
- recurring storms
- persistent fog
- regional temperature patterns

These observations indirectly suggest mountains, valleys, rivers, lakes, forests, and deserts.

Players possess only a limited number of balloons and must strategically decide which outposts host them, allowing intelligence gathering to gradually shift across the frontier.

Additional future facilities may include:
- Observatory
- Telegraph Office
- Geological Survey Office
- Tribal Embassy
- Botanical Garden

Together these facilities form an expanding intelligence network supporting future expeditions while preserving uncertainty.
